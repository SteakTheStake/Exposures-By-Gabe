About

Narratives in ambient light

Welcome to Exposures By Gabe. I'm passionate about capturing the beauty of the natural world and sharing moments that inspire and move people. Each photograph tells a story, whether it's the beautiful to look at or something that makes you think.

My work focuses on landscape, urban environments, and portrait photography, I'm always seeking to find the little oversaught details in ordinary life and to showcase the complexities of the world.
