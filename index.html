About

Narratives in ambient light

Welcome to Exposures By Gabe. I'm passionate about capturing the beauty of the natural world and sharing moments that inspire and move people. Each photograph tells a story, whether it's the serene tranquility of a mountain landscape or the dynamic energy of ocean waves.

My work focuses on landscape, urbex, and portrait photography--always seeking to find the extraordinary in the ordinary and to showcase the incredible diversity of our planet's textures and moods.
